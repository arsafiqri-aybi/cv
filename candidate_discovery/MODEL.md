# Candidate Discovery Interview Model v1.0

## Purpose

Build a reusable Master Candidate Evidence Base through a conversational interview that is adaptive, non-repetitive, bounded, and reusable across future applications.

## Two modes

### Master Discovery
Capture broadly reusable candidate evidence.

### Target Delta
For a specific target application, ask only missing information that could materially improve requirement coverage, claim calibration, transferability, evidence strength, or hard-constraint interpretation.

Do not repeat Master Discovery for every company.

## Coverage regions

The system can capture identity/professional context, career direction, employment, projects, education, certifications, leadership/volunteering, research/publications, awards, skills/tools/domain exposure, tasks, contribution, ownership, outputs, outcomes, scope, metrics, collaboration, problem solving, artifacts, provenance, chronology, constraints, and uncertainty.

These are coverage regions, not mandatory interview sections.

## Episode

An episode is a bounded unit such as a job, internship, project, education period, certification, volunteer activity, research/publication, or award.
