## Membership rule
Primary region is a retrieval/indexing decision, not a claim of ontological isolation.

## Promotion rule
A construct remains in v1 only if it has a clear operational definition, is distinguishable from neighbors, improves reasoning/retrieval/validation/output, is not merely stylistic synonymy, and preserves protected non-edges.
