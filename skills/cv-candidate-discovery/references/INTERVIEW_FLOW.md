# Interview Flow v1.0

D0 State load — load prior candidate evidence, interview state, preferences, and target application.

D1 Timeline sweep — discover major work, project, education, certification, and other material episodes without deep probing.

D2 Episode completion — prioritize useful episodes, ask bounded probes, mark CV_USABLE or BLOCKED, then move on.

D3 Cross-episode pass — capture reusable skills, tools, domains, credentials, collaboration, languages, and portfolio links.

D4 Outcome/metric pass — ask only for strong episodes where a reliable outcome/metric may exist. No repeated pressure for numbers.

D5 Provenance pass — capture repositories, portfolio, live products, certificates, publications, and other artifacts.

D6 Target delta — when a concrete target exists, ask only unresolved material P2 gaps.

D7 Consistency — resolve material date/title/ownership conflicts.

D8 Close — stop when no P0 gap remains, material episodes are CV_USABLE, and target gaps are answered or explicitly unresolved.

Do not deeply exhaust one episode before mapping the rest of the candidate history.
