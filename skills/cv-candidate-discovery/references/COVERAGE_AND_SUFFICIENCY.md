# Evidence Coverage and Sufficiency v1.0

## Coverage states

UNSEEN — not discussed.
DISCOVERED — episode exists but is too thin for safe CV use.
PARTIAL — useful information exists but material gaps remain.
CV_USABLE — enough information exists to write at least one truthful, non-misleading CV statement or entry.
TARGET_READY — enough information exists for the current target application.
BLOCKED — a contradiction or missing material fact prevents safe use.

## Minimum sufficient episode

Normally CV_USABLE when enough is known about:
- episode identity;
- truthful date precision;
- role/context when relevant;
- at least one meaningful task or contribution;
- ownership;
- output, outcome, or meaningful responsibility;
- provenance status.

Not every episode needs a metric, leadership story, failure story, or exhaustive tool list.

## Stop rule

Stop probing when the episode is CV_USABLE, no P0/P1 gap remains, no current target requires material P2 clarification, and another answer is unlikely to change factual integrity, claim ceiling, target relevance, evidence selection, or hard-constraint status.

## Metrics

Ask once when a reliable metric is plausibly available and useful. If the candidate says no reliable metric exists, record that and do not ask again unless new evidence appears.

More detail is optional after sufficiency.
