# Application Targeting Governor

## Objective

Spend tailoring effort only where it can materially improve relevance/interpretation without weakening truth.

## Effort order

1. verify target vacancy;
2. identify critical/high requirements;
3. retrieve strongest candidate evidence;
4. diagnose material gaps;
5. tailor high-impact sections/evidence;
6. use company context only when it changes a decision;
7. stop when remaining changes are cosmetic.

## Do not over-personalize

Stop company-specific research when:
- new facts do not change evidence selection;
- new facts do not change terminology/context;
- new facts do not affect constraints;
- content would become employer flattery rather than evidence.

## Reuse

Reuse:
- candidate evidence;
- stable role-family knowledge;
- still-valid mappings.

Do not blindly reuse:
- stale vacancy requirements;
- company-specific terminology;
- current technology/platform assumptions.

## Feedback

A rejection or interview does not prove the targeting strategy caused the outcome.

Use application feedback as a bounded hypothesis signal, not scientific truth.
