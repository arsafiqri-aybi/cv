# Company Context Policy v1.0

## Principle

Company research serves **relevance**, not flattery.

## Useful company context

Retrieve only when material:
- industry/domain;
- product/service category;
- customer type;
- technology stack when publicly and reliably known;
- regulatory environment;
- market/business model when role-relevant;
- team/function context;
- company-specific terminology tied to real work.

## Weak/non-diagnostic context

Usually do not encode in CV:
- generic values;
- slogans;
- employer-brand adjectives;
- broad mission statements;
- social-media marketing language.

These may matter more to a cover letter/interview than to evidence selection in the CV.

## Truth boundary

Do not infer:
- candidate shares company values;
- candidate knows internal systems;
- candidate has used a tool just because company uses it;
- candidate fits culture.

## Freshness

Company/vacancy context can change.

For concrete applications:
- verify current posting/source;
- record source date/freshness;
- prefer the exact active vacancy over generic role pages.

## Missing company context

Do not block a strong vacancy-targeted CV when company context is unavailable.

Fallback:
```text
vacancy requirements
→ target role
→ candidate evidence
```

Company context is optional unless it materially affects the application.
