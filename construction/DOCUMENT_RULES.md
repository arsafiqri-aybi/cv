# Document Rules v1.1

Canonical editorial standards:
- `standards/CONSISTENCY_STANDARD.md`
- `standards/DATE_STANDARD.md`

## Hard rules

1. Do not fabricate candidate facts.
2. Do not fabricate metrics.
3. Do not change or invent dates to hide gaps or create false precision.
4. Do not imply sole ownership for shared work.
5. Do not list unsupported skills as proven capability.
6. Do not use invisible keyword stuffing.
7. Do not claim a universal ATS score.
8. Do not encode critical text only in images.
9. Do not add demographic/sensitive data by default unless context legitimately requires it.
10. Do not sacrifice legibility for visual novelty.
11. Do not mix canonical section identities even when display labels vary.
12. Do not use inconsistent display date separators/languages without a reason.

## Contextual defaults

Defaults, not laws:
- reverse chronology for conventional industry experience;
- concise bullets;
- canonical date standard;
- explicit semantic section headings;
- conventional legible fonts;
- restrained styling;
- selectable-text PDF;
- single-column when platform is unknown and robustness is prioritized.

## Exception handling

Creative/academic/regional contexts may require different structures.

When deviating, record the reason:
- occupational convention;
- evidence density;
- jurisdiction;
- academic record;
- portfolio dependency;
- machine constraints;
- localization.
