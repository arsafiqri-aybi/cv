# Known Limitations — v1.1.0

## Scientific
- Literature mapping is breadth-first, not a systematic review.
- Scientific saturation is not claimed.
- Modern AI/LLM hiring findings are emerging and system-specific.
- Holistic CV criterion validity remains an important open question.
- Achievement/quantification diagnosticity is not fully established.
- Tailoring evidence is not isolated from writing-quality effects across all contexts.

## Document structure
- Typography numeric ranges are engineering defaults, not validated universal optima.
- Production ATS/parser behavior remains system-specific and often proprietary.
- Multi-column robustness must be tested on the actual artifact.
- Tagged-PDF quality depends on the production toolchain.
- Academic CVs and highly creative occupations need more specialized structure profiles.

## Context
- Cross-cultural conventions are under-developed.
- Legal requirements are jurisdiction-specific.
- Localization rules beyond English/Indonesian examples are not exhaustive.

## Evaluation
- Root and document-structure adversarial cases are test definitions, not independent recruiter validation.
- Real-world callback outcomes are noisy and not causal proof.

## Runtime
- The system cannot verify facts absent from user/source evidence.
- It cannot guarantee interviews, offers, performance, parsing, or ranking.
- Final artifact parseability must be tested on the exported file.

## Release claim

v1.1.0 means the operational architecture, document-structure subsystem, consistency standards, schemas, validators, and automated repository checks exist.

It does not mean universal scientific completeness.
