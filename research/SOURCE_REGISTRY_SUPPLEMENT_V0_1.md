# Source Registry Supplement v0.1

Verified during 2026-10-06 breadth pass.

## S15 — Recruitment and selection synthesis

Potočnik, K., Anderson, N. R., Born, M., Kleinmann, M., & Nikolaou, I. (2021).  
**Paving the way for research in recruitment and selection: recent developments, challenges and future opportunities.**  
European Journal of Work and Organizational Psychology, 30(2), 159–174.  
DOI: 10.1080/1359432X.2021.1904898

Use:
- broader recruitment + selection umbrella;
- technology as a distinct modern research region;
- applicant reactions and impression management.

Evidence: integrative review of roughly 40 meta-analyses/reviews.

Evidence: integrative Annual Review.

## S17 — Skill signals experiment

Piopiunik, M., Schwerdt, G., Simon, L., & Woessmann, L. (2020).  
**Skills, signals, and employability: An experimental investigation.**  
European Economic Review, 123, 103374.  
DOI: 10.1016/j.euroecorev.2020.103374

Use:
- causal evidence that randomized CV skill signals affect interview invitation choices;
- context-dependence of signal value.

Evidence: randomized lab-in-the-field experiment with HR managers.

## S18 — Fit meta-analysis

Kristof-Brown, A. L., Zimmerman, R. D., & Johnson, E. C. (2005).  
**Consequences of individuals' fit at work: A meta-analysis of person–job, person–organization, person–group, and person–supervisor fit.**  
Personnel Psychology, 58, 281–342.  
DOI: 10.1111/j.1744-6570.2005.00672.x

Use:
- fit distinctions;
- preentry and postentry outcomes.

Evidence: meta-analysis, 172 studies / 836 effects.

## S20 — Résumé inference pathways

## S21 — Layout experiment

Arnulf, J. K., Tegner, L., & Larssen, Ø. (2010).  
**Impression making by résumé layout: Its impact on the probability of being shortlisted.**  
European Journal of Work and Organizational Psychology, 19(2), 221–230.  
DOI: 10.1080/13594320902903613

Use:
- layout affects screening judgments.

Boundary:
- small sample;
- older context;
- not proof of universal layout rule.

## S27 — Résumé composition / modern signaling

International Journal of Selection and Assessment, 33, e70022.  
DOI: 10.1111/ijsa.70022

Use:
- detail, clarity, structure;
- tailoring;
- interview ratio;
- days in job search;

## S28 — AI screening bias

**ChatGPT is a gender bias echo-chamber in HR recruitment: an NLP analysis and framework to uncover the language roots of bias.**  
AI & Society (2025).  
DOI: 10.1007/s00146-025-02564-8

Use:
- emerging evidence that LLM-based résumé screening may echo gender-coded signals from job descriptions and candidate names/attributes.

Boundary:
- specific model/setup/data;
- do not generalize to all AI hiring systems.

## S29 — Digital/social assessment validity comparison

Mönke, F. W., Roulin, N., Lievens, F., Bartossek, M. T., & Schäpers, P. (2024).  
**Validity of Social Media Assessments in Personnel Selection: A Systematic Review of the Initial Evidence.**  
European Journal of Psychological Assessment, 40(6), 445–460.  
DOI: 10.1027/1015-5759/a000835

Use:
- shows that more information-rich digital applicant data do not automatically create valid assessment;
- single-rater reliability often poor; job-outcome validity small/near zero in initial evidence.

Evidence: systematic review.
