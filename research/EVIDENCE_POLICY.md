# Evidence Policy

## Purpose

Prevent attractive résumé advice from becoming a system rule without adequate support.

## Claim lifecycle

```text
OBSERVATION
→ CANDIDATE CLAIM
→ RESEARCH CLAIM
→ SCREENED CLAIM
→ PROVISIONAL RULE
→ ACCEPTED RULE
→ DEPRECATED / REVISED
```

Not every observation becomes a rule.

## Evidence classes

### A — Strong synthesis

- meta-analysis;
- systematic review;
- high-quality integrative review.

### B — Strong primary

- field experiment;
- longitudinal field study;
- preregistered multi-study design;
- validated technical audit with realistic data.

### C — Moderate primary

- controlled experiment;
- observational study with meaningful controls;
- construct-validation study.

### D — Professional / regulatory

- professional standards;
- official guidance;
- legal requirements.

These are authoritative for their scope but not universal scientific laws.

### E — Industry observational

Useful for current practice patterns when:

- sample is disclosed;
- collection method is disclosed;
- outcome is defined;
- confounding limitations are acknowledged.

### F — Expert heuristic

Practice advice without strong empirical support.

May guide testing but must not be labeled scientific truth.

## Promotion requirements

A rule can become **accepted** only when:

1. construct is clearly defined;
2. outcome is clearly defined;
3. target context is stated;
4. evidence type is recorded;
5. contradictory evidence is checked;
6. boundary conditions are recorded;
7. causal language matches the study design;
8. no stronger source materially overturns it.

## Outcome taxonomy

Never collapse these into one "CV effectiveness" variable:

- parse success;
- recruiter attention;
- recruiter rating;
- perceived fit;
- shortlist probability;
- interview probability;
- offer probability;
- time to job;
- job performance;
- training performance;
- turnover;
- applicant fairness perception.

## Causal discipline

Examples:

Bad:
> Clear bullets cause better job performance.

Allowed:
> In a 2025 field study, resumes/cover letters rated higher on detail, clarity, and structure were associated with more interview success after controls.

Bad:
> Three years of relevant experience proves competence.

Allowed:
> Experience is a candidate-history signal; its predictive value depends on how experience is operationalized and on the outcome.

## Research freshness

Recheck regularly:

- ATS / recruitment-platform behavior;
- AI screening methods;
- labor-market practices;
- laws and regulations;
- document parsing;
- generative-AI effects on signaling.

Foundational theories may remain useful but should still be tested against modern evidence.

## Contradictions

Contradictions are first-class records.

Do not resolve disagreement by:

- choosing the newest paper automatically;
- choosing the result that matches project assumptions;
- averaging incomparable outcomes;
- hiding null results.

Record:

- conflicting claim;
- source designs;
- samples;
- outcomes;
- possible moderators;
- unresolved status.

## Scientific non-edges

Explicitly preserve important distinctions:

- interview success **is not** job-performance validity;
- writing quality **is not** capability;
- experience duration **is not** achievement quality;
- recruiter fit perception **is not** objective fit;
- ATS parsing **is not** ATS ranking;
- keyword overlap **is not** semantic competence;
- quantified result **is not** automatically stronger evidence;
- beautiful formatting **is not** universal résumé effectiveness.
