# Factual Integrity Audit v1

## Critical checks

For each material statement:

1. What evidence record supports it?
2. Is ownership accurate?
3. Is the verb strength justified?
4. Is a metric real and sourced?
5. Is chronology consistent?
6. Is causal language justified?
7. Did compression remove a material caveat?
8. Does the statement conflict with another section?
9. Is a listed skill supported somewhere?
10. Is any credential misnamed or overstated?

## Severity

### CRITICAL
- fabricated fact/metric/credential;
- false ownership;
- hidden hard-constraint issue;
- materially false chronology.

### HIGH
- causal overclaim;
- unsupported mastery;
- misleading scope;
- contradiction.

### MEDIUM
- vague provenance;
- ambiguous attribution;
- stale/uncertain terminology.

### LOW
- minor wording or formatting issue without factual impact.

No final CV may ship with CRITICAL integrity failures.
